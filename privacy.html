Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Who we are

MHC Diecast (mhcdiecast.com) is an online diecast store run from Bicholim, Goa, India, where MHC Diecast and a small number of independent sellers list cars for sale. Contact: +91 73584 25108 (WhatsApp).

What we collect

  • From Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo. We never see your Google password.
  • From you: mobile number, delivery addresses, the cars in your cart, and the orders you place.
  • Payment screenshots and UPI reference numbers you upload to prove payment. We do not collect bank, card or UPI PIN details — you pay in your own UPI app.
  • On your device: your browser stores your sign-in, cart and light/dark theme choice so the site works. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To show your account, keep your cart in sync across devices, and fill in checkout for you.
  • To process and deliver orders, and to send order updates on WhatsApp.
  • To verify payments and handle refunds, returns or questions.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The seller of your order sees your name, phone, email, delivery address, the items, and your payment screenshot — only for orders placed with them.
  • The MHC Diecast admin can see all orders to manage the store.
  • Service providers that run the site: Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Google (sign-in).
  • Authorities, if required by law.

How long we keep it

Your account data stays until you delete your account. Order records are kept for the seller's accounting and for resolving disputes, even after an account is deleted (they are then no longer linked to your account).

Your choices

  • Edit your name, phone and addresses anytime in My account.
  • Delete your account from My account → Profile → Delete my account. This removes your profile, saved addresses and cart.
  • For any other request about your data, message us on WhatsApp at +91 73584 25108.
