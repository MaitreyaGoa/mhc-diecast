Shipping

  • Anywhere in Goa: free shipping. Pincodes starting with 403 are applied automatically at checkout.
  • Outside Goa: a flat shipping charge of — per order, shown at checkout before you pay.
  • Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 working days after your payment is confirmed.
  • Every car is packed in a box with protective padding so blister cards stay safe in transit.
  • Once shipped, you'll get the courier/tracking number on WhatsApp and on the Track order page.

How payment works

  1. Add cars to your cart and enter your delivery address.
  2. Scan the UPI QR (amount is filled in automatically) and pay with any UPI app.
  3. Upload the payment screenshot. Your car is reserved for you right away.
  4. We verify the payment and confirm your order — usually within a few hours.

If we can't verify a payment, we'll contact you on WhatsApp before cancelling anything.

Returns & refunds

  • Diecast cars are collectibles, so we don't accept returns for change of mind.
  • Damaged in transit or wrong car received? Message us within 48 hours of delivery with an unboxing video and photos. We'll replace it or refund you in full.
  • Minor wear on blister cards/packaging from the factory isn't counted as damage unless mentioned in the listing.
  • If an order is cancelled from our side (e.g. stock issue), the full amount is refunded to the same UPI account within 2–3 working days.
