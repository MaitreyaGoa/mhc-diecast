Auctions

Collectors list a car, everyone bids, highest bid at the end time wins.

How auctions work
  1. Sign in with Google. Anyone can list one car with one photo, a starting bid, a minimum increment, an optional Buy Now price and an end time.
  2. Each new bid must be at least the current bid + the minimum increment (the first bid at least the starting bid). Lower bids are rejected automatically.
  3. Final 2 minutes: each new bid must be at least ₹100 higher (or the seller's increment if bigger), and every bid resets the clock to 2 minutes. The auction ends after 2 minutes with no new bid — so last-second sniping doesn't work.
  4. You can't bid on your own auction or outbid yourself. Bids can't be withdrawn.
  5. Buy Now ends the auction immediately at that price.
  6. At the end time bidding closes and the highest bidder wins. The winner and seller then see each other's contact details.
  7. Payment and delivery are arranged directly between the winner and the seller. MHC Diecast only hosts the auction.
